[b][h1]Dynamic Worlds[/h1][/b]
[i]Fresh terrain, same playthrough.[/i]

Dynamic Worlds is a tModLoader utility mod that regenerates Terraria terrain without making you throw away the run built on top of it.

It snapshots world progression and protected content, generates a fresh world, restores the important parts, and drops you back into the playthrough instead of forcing a restart.

---

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Regenerates the world while preserving progression and protected content
[*] Restores Hardmode, ores, bosses, invasions, world identity, and other major world markers
[*] Restores preserved NPC roster, names, positions, housing, and player spawn handling
[*] Repairs restored vanilla pylons and supports biome-aware pylon relocation with Biome Dowser
[*] Creates rolling pre-regen backups before regen runs
[*] Supports optional scheduled regen in [b]in-game days[/b] or [b]real-world days[/b]
[*] Includes built-in Calamity progression and world-state compatibility
[/list]

---

[h2]Preservation Tools[/h2]

[b]Reality Anchor[/b]
[list]
[*] Preserves exact tiles, walls, liquids, wires, slopes, actuators, and preserved containers
[*] Left-click to toggle a tile
[*] Click and drag to anchor a rectangle
[/list]

[b]Reality Eraser[/b]
[list]
[*] Marks spaces that should stay empty after regen
[*] Good for tunnels, arenas, shafts, and access routes
[*] Left-click to toggle a tile or drag a rectangle
[/list]

[b]Structure Anchor[/b]
[list]
[*] Preserves a whole build as one zone
[*] Click and drag to create a zone
[*] Shift-click inside a zone to remove it
[/list]

[b]Biome Dowser[/b]
[list]
[*] Preserves vanilla pylon structures and relocates them into valid biomes
[*] Right-click opens the settings panel
[*] Supports pylon-specific placement strategies like surface, underground, floating, and ocean variants where appropriate
[/list]

Hold any world tool to see anchors, erasures, structure zones, and Biome Dowser zones together.

[h2]Single Player and Multiplayer[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Single-player:[/b] [code]/regenworld[/code] saves, opens the regen loading flow, restores preserved data, and loads you back in automatically
[*] [b]Multiplayer:[/b] synced tools and zones, admin-controlled [code]/regenworld[/code], permissions, countdown warnings, disconnect-and-reconnect regen flow, and optional scheduled server regen
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Works best when the same mod list stays installed before and after regen
[*] Modded items in preserved containers are supported through tModLoader serialization
[*] Modded tiles, walls, and town NPCs often survive if their source mods stay installed
[*] Compatibility is weaker for custom pylons, unusual tile entities, and major worldgen overhauls
[*] Biome Dowser currently supports vanilla pylons only
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]

[b]Does regen happen while I keep playing?[/b]
No. The mod saves first, regenerates, restores preserved data, and then loads you back in. In multiplayer, players reconnect after the server finishes regen.

[b]Can I schedule regen by real-world days?[/b]
Yes.

[b]Can I use this on multiplayer servers?[/b]
Yes.

[b]Does it work with older worlds?[/b]
If the world still loads in your current Terraria/tModLoader setup, Dynamic Worlds can snapshot and regenerate it.

[b]Can it change my world evil?[/b]
Yes. Turn off [b]Preserve Evil Type[/b] if you want regen to keep the new evil instead of forcing the old one back.
